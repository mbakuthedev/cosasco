The SafeGuard Service Valve

SafeGuard, Cosasco’s latest innovation, features double isolation valve technology to realize the highest safety standards in the world, acting as an airlock against hazardous materials.

The valve is fire-rated and PED-certified. It is made in NACE-compliant 4130 or duplex stainless steel, and it is the lightest service valve Cosasco offers in this class.
