Pulp & Paper

Cosasco measures the direct effect of airborne, internal, and external corrosion in real time. That lets pulp and paper manufacturers:

  • Reduce equipment replacement costs through tighter control of atmospheric corrosion
  • Cut unplanned maintenance by seeing the corrosion rate at critical assets
  • Optimize production by measuring corrosion at each stage of the process
