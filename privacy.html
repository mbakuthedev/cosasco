Privacy Policy

What this recreation keeps, and where it stays.

This local recreation of the Cosasco public website stores two kinds of information, and only in your browser.

Quote list

Products you add to a quote are saved in local storage on this computer. Nothing is sent to a server. Clearing the list, or clearing site data in the browser, removes it.

Consultation forms

The contact and consultation forms do not transmit what you type. After you submit, the page shows a confirmation and keeps the note on this device only. The phone numbers and email addresses published here are the public Cosasco contacts.
